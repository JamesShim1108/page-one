# Unit 2 source-conflict log

This log records conflicts and limits identified while reading the supplied classroom materials. The publication rule is simple: a doubtful assertion is qualified, replaced with a supported example, or withheld. The supplied AMSCO and slide PDFs remain private class sources; they are not copied into `docs/`.
